# Bottom Blueprint Accuracy Method v1.2

The Observatory exists to determine whether the Bottom Blueprint remains useful, not merely to display its dates.

## Timing experiment
Record:
- published bottom center/window;
- current live day and state;
- actual cycle low date/price when it becomes knowable;
- center error in days;
- window hit/miss;
- momentum center/window;
- 5% above 200DMA rule state, streak starts/restarts, first confirmed 45-day streak;
- momentum center error and window hit/miss.

## Candidate cohort experiment
When the Research Bee hands off a candidate, freeze:
- token identity;
- research timestamp;
- Blueprint family;
- archetype basket labels;
- narrative state;
- Core Quality and Timing scores;
- contract/liquidity evidence;
- benchmark snapshot.

Then append performance observations from the research timestamp through the end of the bull cycle or the fifth-halving boundary, whichever is reached first.

### BTC-outperformance measurement
For every candidate eligible for the fourth-halving BTC-outperformer study, store token return and BTC return over the **same eligible lifetime period** and calculate excess return as percentage-point subtraction. Do not substitute a different period for BTC.

## Anti-hindsight rule
Performance can evaluate a past research decision but can never edit the original research snapshot or archetype assignment.

## Accuracy outputs
The Observatory reports descriptive experiment results only while live. A separate future Blueprint Auditor can calculate calibration, hit rates, cohort discrimination, cycle-to-cycle robustness and model error after enough observations exist.
